# Welcome-screen people photos (optional)

Drop up to six square-ish JPEGs here named `person_1.jpg` ... `person_6.jpg` and
the welcome map will show them as the people gathered at each pin instead of the
illustrated avatars. Any missing file falls back to an illustrated avatar.

Only use photos you have the right to use **and** consent from the person for
this purpose (a shoot with models, or members who agreed). Do not use random
stock photos or scraped images, and do not caption them as "members near you":
they are illustration, not live activity.

Tips: face centred, at least 256x256, JPEG under ~150 KB each.
