Child safety standards

Nomad Mingle has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM). This page sets out our standards and how to report a concern.

Our policy

Nomad Mingle is a social app for adults to find people and make plans together. We do not allow any content or behaviour that sexually exploits, abuses or endangers children, anywhere in the app, and we do not permit anyone to use Nomad Mingle to find, groom, contact or exploit a child.

These standards apply to profiles, photos, plans, group chats, direct messages and every other part of the service.

Nomad Mingle is for adults aged 18 or older

The service is intended only for people aged 18 or older. Creating a profile requires a date of birth, and the server checks that the person is at least 18 before the profile is created. We do not knowingly permit anyone under 18 to hold an account. If we learn that an account belongs to a person under 18, we will restrict or remove it.

What is prohibited

  • Any child sexual abuse material (CSAM), in any form, including photos, text or links to such material.
  • Sexualised content involving a minor, or content that sexualises or exploits a child in any way.
  • Grooming: contacting or befriending a minor, or someone presenting as one, for sexual purposes.
  • Sexual extortion of any person, including threats to share intimate images.
  • Soliciting, advertising, trading or encouraging any of the above, including through plans, profiles or chats.

Accounts, messages, photos or plans that break these standards are removed, and the people responsible are removed from the service.

How to report a concern

In the app. You can report a person, a plan or a message from the app. Choose the report option, pick the closest reason (spam, harassment, unsafe, inappropriate, or other), and add details. When you report a message, the app keeps a copy of the message text so a reviewer can see what was said even if it is later changed or deleted. You can also block any member at any time so they can no longer interact with you.

By email. You can also send a concern to cortiqx@gmail.com. Tell us what you saw, where in the app you saw it (the plan, chat or profile) and the name shown on the account. Please do not forward or attach sexual images of anyone under 18: describe where we can find the content instead.

If a child is in immediate danger

Contact your local emergency services or police first. In India you can also report online child sexual abuse material at cybercrime.gov.in. In other countries, use your national child-protection hotline or law enforcement.

How we respond

  • Review. Reports are reviewed by Nomad Mingle's moderators. Concerns about child safety are treated as the highest priority.
  • Action. Moderators can hide messages, mute or suspend accounts, and cancel plans. Accounts involved in CSAE are suspended and removed, and we keep the relevant records so that the matter can be investigated.
  • Reporting to authorities. Where we become aware of apparent CSAM or of a child at risk, we will report it to the appropriate authorities and cooperate with lawful requests from law enforcement, as required by applicable law.
  • Prevention. We review these standards as the service grows and update this page when they change.

Deleting an account does not stop us from keeping safety reports and moderation evidence where needed to investigate abuse or comply with law. See the Delete Account page and the Privacy Policy for details.

Contact

Child safety contact

For child safety concerns, questions about these standards, or requests from child-protection organisations and law enforcement, email cortiqx@gmail.com.
