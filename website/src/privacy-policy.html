3. How we use information

We use personal information only as reasonably necessary to provide and protect the service, including to:

  • create and secure accounts, authenticate members, and verify the minimum-age requirement;
  • display profiles and plans, support memberships, and enable permitted conversations;
  • calculate nearby results when you ask for them and show you to others only if you opt in to discoverability;
  • send service-related notices and manage notification preferences;
  • review reports, enforce community standards, prevent fraud, spam, misuse and security incidents;
  • diagnose service failures, maintain infrastructure, respond to support requests, and comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information. The current mobile application does not activate advertising or analytics tracking. This may change only if the app is updated and this Policy is revised as required.

5. Location, discoverability and contacts

Location access is optional. Your device obtains location only while the app is in use and after you request a location-based action; the app does not request background location or continuously track you. A nearby search sends its search coordinates to the API for that request. If you opt in to “Show me here”, the API stores only rounded coordinates (approximately 1.1 km precision) for nearby-member discovery. Turning the option off removes the stored discoverability coordinates. Location permission can also be changed in your device settings.

Contacts access is used to let you select a person and open a message composer. Contact names and phone numbers are read locally by the app and are not uploaded to our servers by that feature.

Maps may load map tiles from the provider configured for the app build. That provider may receive technical request information, including the requested map area and your device's network address, under its own privacy terms. The operator should identify the active map provider before publication.

6. Retention and account deletion

We keep personal information only for as long as needed for the purposes described in this Policy, unless a longer period is required or permitted by law. Account deletion is available in the app and is described on this site's Delete Account page.

When a deletion request succeeds, the account's authentication record and active profile records are deleted, along with associated friend/block relationships, notification records, device tokens and rate-limit state. Upcoming plans hosted by the member are cancelled. Membership records and messages are de-identified: names and profile photographs are replaced or removed, but conversation content may remain so that the conversation and safety history are not silently rewritten.

Current additional retention period

There is currently no fixed additional retention period or automatic expiry configured for de-identified chat messages, moderation/report records, or uploaded image objects after account deletion. They may remain until the operator determines they are no longer needed for safety, legal claims, compliance or service integrity. A defined maximum period has not yet been adopted. The operator must set and implement one before relying on this Policy as a final store disclosure.

Deletion removes image references from active profile records, but the current account-deletion process does not explicitly purge the corresponding Cloud Storage image files. Accordingly, image objects may remain after a deletion request. We will update this section if the process or retention schedule changes.

7. Your choices and privacy rights

  • Profile and preferences: review or update editable profile and notification settings in the app.
  • Location: deny device permission, use city browsing, or turn off “Show me here” to remove stored discoverability coordinates.
  • Access, correction or objection: where applicable law provides, request access to, correction of, restriction of, objection to, or portability of your personal information.
  • Deletion: delete your account in the app. If you cannot sign in, contact us using the address in section 10 and include the account email or other information we reasonably need to locate the account. We may need to verify your identity before acting.

Rights and response deadlines depend on where you live. You may also have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority. We will not discriminate against you for exercising a right provided by applicable law.
